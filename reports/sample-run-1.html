Question

Why did cardiovascular device recall records rise between 2023 and 2024, and which product codes drove it?

Verified answer

Cardiovascular device recall records rose from 281 in 2023 to 482 in 2024, an increase of 201 records. The three product codes with the largest increases — DSY, DQO, and OEZ — together added 131 records, accounting for 65.2% of the total rise. DSY contributed 53 records from a single event, with the top root cause Equipment maintenance and the top recalling firm Atrium Medical Corporation. DQO contributed 49 records from 3 events involving 3 firms, with the top root cause Under Investigation by firm and the top recalling firm Boston Scientific Corporation. OEZ contributed 29 records from 21 events involving 6 firms, with the top root cause Process control and the top recalling firm MEDLINE INDUSTRIES, LP - Northfield. Additional increases came from DQK, which added 25 records from 4 events involving 4 firms, with the top root cause Under Investigation by firm and the top recalling firm Boston Scientific Corporation, and DPT, which added 22 records from a single event, with the top root cause Process control and the top recalling firm Smiths Medical ASD, Inc.
