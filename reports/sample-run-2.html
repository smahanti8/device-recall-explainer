Question

Which three product codes drove the increase in cardiovascular device recalls from 2023 to 2024, and what share of the increase do they account for?

Verified answer

Cardiovascular device recall records rose from 281 in 2023 to 482 in 2024, an increase of 201 records. The top three product codes — DSY, DQO, and OEZ — together added 131 records, accounting for 65.2% of the total increase. DSY contributed 53 recall‑record increase from a single event; its 2024 total was 54 records, the top root cause was Equipment maintenance and the top recalling firm was Atrium Medical Corporation. DQO contributed 49 recall‑record increase from 3 events; its 2024 total was 51 records, the top root cause was Under Investigation by firm and the top recalling firm was Boston Scientific Corporation. OEZ contributed 29 recall‑record increase from 21 events; its 2024 total was 43 records, the top root cause was Process control and the top recalling firm was MEDLINE INDUSTRIES, LP - Northfield.
